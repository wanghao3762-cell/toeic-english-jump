## Project business-form additions

- `extensions.json` contains 20 explicitly project-selected additions, including
  meeting, sales, shipping, minutes, premises and building. A source lemma list
  does not necessarily separately list every derived or inflected noun form.
- Source is `PROJECT`, no official rank claimed; appended to route 4. These are
  a practical supplement, not an exhaustive official TOEIC vocabulary list.
- Total now 4129 unique entries: NGSL 2809 + TSL 1250 + NGSL-SUP 50 + PROJECT 20.

## Chinese definitions and phonetics

- ECDICT, https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT
- Download: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/skywind3000/ECDICT/master/ecdict.csv
- MIT license supplied by upstream: `sources/ECDICT-LICENSE.txt`.
- Dictionary meanings are automatically joined to the research word lists and
  shortened for display. They are general dictionary senses, not manually checked
  TOEIC-specific senses. Full dictionary source is retained locally for audit.
  Seven introductory pronouns and three accented TSL forms have project-authored
  gloss corrections in `tools/build_vocab.py`; additional missing fields and calendar
  senses are supplied in `lexical-corrections.json`. Pronunciations are reference
  variants, not a promise to cover all UK/US variants. Generated entries record corrections.

## Original learning content and illustrations

- `memory-content.json`: 110 original bilingual short examples, collocations and
  related-word links. Teaching examples, not ETS exam questions or dictionary quotations.
- `memory.js`: 8 original code-native shared SVG scene illustrations. Each is a
  category/context aid, not a bespoke picture for each word. No remote image loads.
- Remaining 4019 words show a dictionary-only card. Full TOEIC-sense and sentence
  editing for all words remains unfinished and is not implied by nonempty fields.
- Learning evidence and limitations: `LEARNING_RESEARCH.md`.
- Run `python -X utf8 tools/audit_vocab.py`; machine-readable findings are in
  `../qa/vocabulary-audit.json`, including exact source/rank coverage and SHA256 checks.

## Project modifications

The combined vocabulary and learning-stage annotations are distributed under
CC BY-SA 4.0. Meanings and phonetics come from ECDICT. Deduplicated entries,
source rank, source identifier and project stage are generated by
`python tools/build_vocab.py`; reproducibility hashes are in `manifest.json`.

NGSL ranks 1–500, 501–1200, and 1201 onward make up training routes 1–3;
TSL ranks 1–650 and 651 onward make routes 4–5. Route names reference the
learner's A1→C1 goals, but these are project learning groups, NOT official
CEFR assignments for words or proof of proficiency. Common concrete beginner
words are moved to the front of route 1. Each unit contains at most 20 words.
